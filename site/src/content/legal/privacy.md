Welcome to Learning Loons (https://learningloons.com). Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what personal data we collect and how we use it.

### Information We Collect

We may collect:

- Your name and email address (if you sign up for our newsletter)
- Anonymous data such as IP address, browser type, and referring pages (through Google Analytics)
- Information shared through embedded content (e.g., YouTube videos)

### How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

- Improve your experience on our site
- Send occasional updates or newsletters (only if you opt-in)
- Analyze website traffic and performance

### Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services that collect, monitor, and analyze data:

- Google Analytics
- MailerLite (email list management)
- YouTube (video embeds may collect viewer data)

These services have their own privacy policies which you can review on their respective sites.

### Cookies

We use cookies to personalize content and ads, to provide social media features, and to analyze our traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

### Your Rights

You can request:

- Access to any data we have about you
- Corrections or deletion of your personal data
- To unsubscribe from future communications at any time

## Introduction

WhereBabe is a location-sharing mobile application designed for couples to share their real-time locations with each other. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the WhereBabe app.

**Developer:** Learning Loons  
**Contact:** admin@learningloons.com  
**Website:** https://learningloons.com

## Information We Collect

### Location Data

- **Real-time GPS coordinates** (latitude, longitude, accuracy)
- **Location timestamps** indicating when location data was recorded
- **Location sharing status** (whether sharing is enabled or disabled)

### Personal Information

- **Names** you provide during app setup (your name and your partner’s name)
- **Couple identification codes** (short codes like “BLUE42” for connecting partners)
- **Profile photos** (if you choose to add them during setup)

### Technical Information

- **Device identifiers** for Firebase database connections
- **App usage data** (connection status, error logs for troubleshooting)

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Purpose

WhereBabe uses your information solely to provide location-sharing functionality between you and your designated partner. Specifically:

- **Location sharing:** Your real-time location is shared only with your coupled partner
- **Distance calculation:** We calculate and display the distance between you and your partner
- **Connection management:** We maintain your couple connection using identification codes

### Data Processing

- Location data is processed in real-time to provide current positioning
- Historical location data may be temporarily stored for app functionality
- Profile photos are stored locally on your device and optionally shared with your partner

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### Limited Sharing

- **Your location is shared ONLY with your designated partner** through the couple code system
- We do not share, sell, or distribute your personal information to third parties
- We do not use your data for advertising or marketing purposes

### Service Providers

We use Firebase (Google) as our database service provider to store and sync location data between coupled devices. Firebase’s privacy practices are governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

### Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of users or others.

## Data Storage and Security

### Storage Location

- Location data is stored securely in Firebase Realtime Database
- Profile photos are stored locally on your device
- Personal information is encrypted during transmission

### Data Retention

- **Active sharing:** Location data is maintained while you actively use the app
- **Account deletion:** When you delete the app or clear data, your information is removed from our systems
- **Automatic cleanup:** Old location data may be automatically purged after extended inactivity

### Security Measures

- Data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard protocols
- Access to location data is restricted to your coupled partner only
- We implement technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access

## Your Rights and Choices

### Control Over Data

- **Stop sharing:** You can disable location sharing at any time within the app
- **Delete data:** Uninstalling the app removes your data from our systems
- **Update information:** You can change your name, photos, and settings within the app

### Access and Correction

- You can view all data we have about you through the app interface
- You can update or correct your information at any time
- You can request deletion of your data by contacting us

## Children’s Privacy

WhereBabe is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

## Third-Party Services

WhereBabe integrates with:

- **Firebase (Google):** For real-time database and authentication services
- **Google Maps:** For map display and location services
- **Device photo gallery:** For profile photo selection (with your permission)

These services have their own privacy policies governing data use.

## Permissions Required

WhereBabe requests the following device permissions:

- **Location access:** To share your real-time location with your partner
- **Camera and photo access:** To select or take profile photos (optional)
- **Internet access:** To sync data with your partner’s device

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. We will notify users of material changes through the app or by posting the updated policy on our website.

## International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States where Firebase servers are located. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

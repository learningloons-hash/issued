Moody Merlion ([@MOODYMERLION_BOT](https://t.me/moodymerlion_bot)) is a Telegram bot built around the idea that many teenagers already live in chat apps. The product is emotional check-ins and supportive conversation — a mood companion vibe, not a diagnosis engine.

The longer piece on teen mental health, AI companions, and when worry is warranted is in Writing. This page is the bot.

## What it does

- Meets teens on Telegram — no new app install beyond what they already use.
- Offers check-ins and supportive dialogue tuned for emotional weather, not homework.
- Stays in the companion lane: listening, grounding, and gentle prompts.

## What it is not

Not a clinician, crisis line, or replacement for professional care. If someone is unsafe, contact local emergency services or a trusted adult. The essay makes that distinction too.

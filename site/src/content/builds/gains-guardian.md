Gains Guardian (the bot behind [t.me/gains_guardian_bot](https://t.me/gains_guardian_bot)) is a proactive accountability coach. It texts first. That is the whole product.

The essay on Hawthorne effects, implementation intentions, and why January gym plans collapse is in Writing. This page is the tool.

## What it does

- Daily morning check-ins at a time you pick.
- Remembers the goal and the conversation.
- A seven-day trial with no credit card, as advertised on the original post.

It pairs with the later essay on sobriety and momentum: fill the idle hours with something that cannot coexist with the old default. Fitness is one of those somethings. The bot is not a clinic.

## What it is not

Not a trainer in your pocket for medical advice. Not a Blog post. The related longform stays under Mind & meaning; the product lives here.

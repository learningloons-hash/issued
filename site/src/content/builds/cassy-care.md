Cassy Care is a Telegram companion for the confinement period and the first twelve weeks of motherhood. She is not a doctor, a therapist, or a parenting manual. She is meant to be the kind friend who texts first.

The longer argument — why postpartum loneliness is structural, and why passive apps fail — is in the essay. This page is the product.

## What she does

- Starts the conversation. Daily messages, timed to where you and the baby actually are.
- Remembers what you said you were worried about.
- Stays on Telegram. No extra app to open, no group to join.

## What she is not

Cassy is not medical care. If you are in crisis, contact a clinician or local emergency service. The essay is not a substitute for that either.

WhereBabe is a location-sharing app for couples. You connect with a short couple code. Your live location is for that partner, not a feed, not advertisers.

On the old WordPress site, `/wherebabe/` dumped people straight into the privacy policy. That was the product page. This is a real one; the policy is next door and the old URLs still redirect.

## What it collects

Real-time GPS, names you type at setup, a couple code, optional photos stored on device. Processing is there to show where the two of you are and how far apart. The full list is in the [WhereBabe privacy policy](/legal/wherebabe-privacy/).

## What it does not do

It does not sell location data. It does not put the learning blog in the app, and it does not belong in the blog masonry either.
